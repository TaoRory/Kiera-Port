Neuro Legacy

Brand Positioning & Content Strategy — as Social Media Manager & Content Creator, I developed brand positioning and a multi-platform content strategy across 7 channels.

The Challenge

Expertise without a content system

Neuro Legacy was founded to share insights on parenting, family legacy and personal development through coaching and educational content. The founders had deep expertise but lacked a content strategy and social growth system — and needed to turn a long-form podcast into digital assets and a presence across many platforms.

The Solution

One recording, many platforms

  • Content strategy built around the founders' expertise and long-term vision
  • Defined content pillars: parenting, family legacy, personal growth, elite education
  • Edited and optimized long-form podcast episodes for YouTube
  • Repurposed podcasts into short-form video for multiple platforms
  • Managed content distribution across 7 social channels
  • Formatted content specifically for each platform
  • Built a consistent publishing workflow
