Brand Building Social Media Strategy Content Marketing E-Commerce

OrderByDau

Fashion E-Commerce Brand — built and scaled a content-driven SHEIN ordering brand through strategic social media, curated fashion content, and audience-focused marketing.

The Challenge

A crowded SHEIN-ordering market

The SHEIN ordering market in Vietnam is highly competitive — most sellers simply repost product photos and sell directly. OrderByDau needed to feel trustworthy, look beautiful, deliver real value to young shoppers, and stand out from the crowd.

The Solution

Content-first, aesthetic-led positioning

  • Researched what students and young women look for: affordable fashion for special occasions and social media
  • Content-first strategy focused on outfit inspiration rather than product listings
  • Curated fashion collections by aesthetic, color palette, trend and budget
  • Positioned the brand around affordability, trust and seamless shopping
  • Published mix-and-match guides, styling recommendations and trend-based content
  • Built a cohesive, on-brand Instagram presence
  • Ran paid ads to drive conversion and repeat purchases
The Results

From launch to a growing shop.
