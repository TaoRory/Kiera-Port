Alpha College Prep

Community Growth & Student Engagement — as Public Community Management Executive, I built and managed student communities and content strategies that attracted prospects, drove engagement, and supported business growth.

The Challenge

Engagement that drives real outcomes

Alpha College Prep wanted to build a stronger educational community and attract students interested in university admissions, scholarships and career opportunities. The challenge was keeping students engaged, delivering real value, and turning community participation into long-term business growth.

The Solution

Community, content & lead generation in one system

  • Managed a private community for mentees
  • Curated internships, extracurricular activities, scholarships and resources
  • Created motivational content to increase engagement
  • Expanded into public communities and social channels
  • Produced educational content on scholarships, admissions and success stories
  • Ran visual content and community campaigns
  • Built content systems linking community engagement with lead generation
