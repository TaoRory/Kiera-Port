# Nguyen Tue Nhi — Personal Portfolio Website Spec

## 1. Design Direction

**Mục tiêu website:** giới thiệu thương hiệu cá nhân Nguyen Tue Nhi với vai trò Fashion Content Creator, Social Media Manager, Content Strategist, Founder. Đóng vai trò portfolio, công cụ tìm khách hàng/cơ hội nghề, trưng bày case studies, xây thương hiệu cá nhân dài hạn.

**Cảm hứng:** Fashion Editorial Website, Luxury Portfolio, Personal Branding, Modern Magazine Layout.

**Phong cách:** Elegant, Soft, Premium, Feminine, Minimalist, Strategic.

**Brand personality:** Sáng tạo, chuyên nghiệp, có gu thẩm mỹ, hiểu social media & branding, tạo ra kết quả thực tế.

**User perception target:** "Đây không chỉ là content creator mà còn là người có khả năng xây dựng thương hiệu và phát triển social media bài bản."

**Design principles:** tập trung nội dung, typography là yếu tố chính, hình ảnh lớn, khoảng trắng rộng, chuyển động tinh tế, ít hiệu ứng, ưu tiên cảm giác editorial hơn marketing.

## 3. Typography

## 7. Case Study Detail Template

**Common flow:** Page Header (logo NHI + nav) → Breadcrumb "← Back to All Case Studies" → Hero → Project Overview → Challenge → Solution → Mockup → Results → Content Examples → Impact → CTA

### Case Study 01 — Personal TikTok Growth
- **Hero:** 2 cột (45/55). Title "Personal TikTok Growth", desc "Building a personal brand to 15K+ followers and 726K+ likes." Tags: Content Creation, Personal Branding, TikTok Strategy. Right: fashion creator portrait.
- **Overview layout:** 3 cột (Challenge / Phone Mockup / Results)
- **Challenge:** "Starting from 0, I needed to build my personal brand that stands out in the fashion niche and attracts a loyal audience."
- **Solution bullets:**
  - Defined content pillars: Outfit Inspo, Hauls, Styling Tips, Daily Looks
  - Consistent posting & trend-based content
  - High-quality visuals and relatable storytelling
  - Engaged with the community authentically
- **TikTok mockup:** iPhone mockup hiển thị TikTok profile, video grid, profile stats
- **Results (2×2):** 15K+ Followers · 726K+ Likes · 40+ Videos · 3M+ Total Views
- **Top Performing Content:** 5 cards horizontal — 325.3K, 842.4K, 190.4K, 7.5K views + 1 most recent viral
- **Key learnings:** Building a personal brand requires consistency, audience understanding, content experimentation. Continuous testing + trend adaptation → sustainable growth giữ identity rõ ràng trong fashion niche.
- **CTA:** "Interested in growing your brand through content?" + "LET'S WORK TOGETHER →" + "VIEW MORE PROJECTS →"

### Case Study 02 — OrderByDau
- **Hero:** Title "OrderByDau / Fashion E-Commerce Brand". Desc: built and scaled content-driven SHEIN ordering brand qua strategic social media, curated fashion content, audience-focused marketing. Tags: Brand Building, Social Media Strategy, Content Marketing, E-Commerce.
- **Hero image direction:** fashion flatlay, packaging, IG feed, customer feedback, brand moodboard
- **Overview layout:** Challenge / Instagram Mockup / Results
- **Challenge:** thị trường SHEIN ordering ở Việt Nam cạnh tranh cao, đa số chỉ repost sản phẩm và bán trực tiếp. Cần tạo brand đáng tin, đẹp về visual, có giá trị cho young shoppers, đứng nổi bật.
- **Solution bullets:**
  - Research insights học sinh/phụ nữ trẻ tìm thời trang giá tốt cho dịp đặc biệt/social media
  - Content-first strategy, focus outfit inspiration thay vì list sản phẩm
  - Curated fashion collections theo aesthetic, color palette, trend, budget
  - Position quanh affordability, trust, seamless shopping
  - Publish mix-and-match guides, styling rec, trend-based content
  - Cohesive Instagram presence
  - Paid ads để tăng conversion + repeat purchase
- **Instagram mockup:** IG feed, story highlights, bio, story covers
- **Results:** 500+ Monthly Orders · 150+ Content Posts · 1K+ IG Followers · January 2026 (Brand Launch)
- **Brand Impact:** thay vì repost sản phẩm, brand khác biệt qua curated content, styling, customer-centered communication → một trong những SHEIN ordering brands trust nhất niche.
- **Content examples (6):** Outfit Under 500K · Pink Girl Aesthetic Collection · Date Night Outfit Ideas · Back To School Picks · Capsule Wardrobe Essentials · Customer Favorites This Month
- **Customer feedback:** 3 cards (review, UGC photo, testimonial)
- **Business lessons:** brand fashion e-commerce thành công dựa trên trust, aesthetics, customer experience — không chỉ availability.
- **CTA:** "Explore the Brand" + "VIEW BRAND INSTAGRAM →" + "VIEW MORE PROJECTS →"

## 9. Responsive Design

### Breakpoints
| Device | Width | Container | Grid | Section spacing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desktop | 1440px | 1280px | 12 cols | 140px |
| Tablet | 768px | 90% | 8 cols | 100px |
| Mobile | 390px | 24px padding | 4 cols | 72px |

### Responsive rules
- **Hero:** Desktop 40/60 → Tablet stack (text trên, image dưới) → Mobile stack (image trên, text dưới)
- **About:** Desktop 50/50 → Tablet stack → Mobile stack (3 ảnh chuyển carousel)
- **Featured Content:** 3 → 2 → 1 cột
- **Case Studies:** 2×2 → 2 cột → 1 cột
- **Services:** 3 → 2 → 1 cột
- **Timeline:** Horizontal → Vertical → Vertical

### Typography scaling
| Element | Desktop | Tablet | Mobile |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | 72px | 56px | 42px |
| H2 | 48px | 40px | 32px |
| H3 | 32px | 28px | 24px |
| Body | 16px | 16px | 15px |

## 10. Micro Interactions

- **Navbar on scroll:** background transparent → solid, 300ms
- **Buttons hover:** translateY(-2px), 200ms
- **Case study cards hover:** scale 1.02, image zoom 1.05, 250ms
- **Content cards hover:** play button xuất hiện, thumbnail tối nhẹ, 250ms
- **Metrics scroll animation:** count-up (0 → 15K+, 0 → 726K+, 0 → 500+, 0 → 1B+), 1500ms
- **Images hover:** zoom nhẹ, 300ms
- **Page transition:** Fade + Up, 500ms
- **Scroll reveal:** opacity 0→100, translateY 30px→0, 600ms
